GUIDELINES

Community Guidelines

You can't do the following on Plato:

Exploit or interfere with Plato or another user's experience.
Win-trade or manipulate the ranking system.
Use third-party programs to gain an unfair advantage.
Buy or sell accounts for real money.

Public Room Chat Guidelines

No obscene, sexual, pornographic, or illegal content.
Do not share another user's personal or confidential information.
Do not spam or disrupt the flow of chat.
Do not encourage violence, self-harm, suicide, or hate against any group.
Use only the language of the selected server.
Breaking these guidelines may result in a 4 hour chat restriction or further moderation action (permanent ban).

Who are the Leaders?

Leaders are official community representatives who act as a bridge between players and the development team, helping build a fun, safe, and fair environment for everyone.

What are the Leaders' tasks?

  • Reporting bugs and technical issues to the development team.
  • Sharing player feedback, concerns, and suggestions.
  • Proposing ideas to improve the player experience.
  • Reviewing and removing inappropriate profiles or bios.
  • Submitting ban requests for repeated rule violations.
  • Moderating rooms and taking appropriate action.

How to become a leader?

  • Leaders are chosen based on their positive engagement and active involvement in the community.
  • Candidates are nominated by current Leaders and must be approved by the game developer.
New Leaders are appointed when needed, with a maximum of 4 Leaders per server.
